# Test Checklist

Open index.html in a browser (double-click it). Follow the steps in order and check that what happens matches.

## Layer 1: one task

1. Open index.html. The top bar says "Start Up" and money shows $0.
2. There is one row, "Fix a Bug", with a colored square on the left and an empty progress bar. It says "Click to start - pays $10".
3. Click the row. The bar starts filling and the text says "Working...". The row border turns yellow.
4. While it is filling, click the row again. Nothing changes (the bar does not restart).
5. Wait about 10 seconds. The bar finishes, money goes to $10, and the row goes back to "Click to start".
6. Click it again and wait. Money goes to $20.
7. Click it, then switch to another tab for 15 seconds and come back. You were paid $10 (only once, because nobody is hired).
